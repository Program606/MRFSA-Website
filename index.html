What is MRFSA?

A Filipino club at Mount Royal University that celebrates and promotes Filipino culture, heritage, and traditions within the campus community. It provides a welcoming space where students of Filipino descent—and those simply interested in the culture—can connect, share experiences, and foster a sense of belonging.

Community

We create supportive connections where every student feels welcome.

Culture

We celebrate Filipino heritage while embracing the diversity of our wider community.

Growth

We empower members to build skills, confidence, and lifelong connections.

Our Mission

We unite students in celebrating and sharing Filipino culture through social events, traditions, and values, while providing opportunities for cultural learning, leadership, and personal growth.
